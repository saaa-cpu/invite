For Sema 🌸

I made something I'd like to share with you.

Hi Sema,

Before anything else, thank you for opening this little surprise.

I wanted to make something different—something made with time, thought, and appreciation.

Over time you've become someone I genuinely appreciate.

Not because of one big moment, but because of all the small ones.

The conversations.

The laughs.

The ordinary days that somehow became better.

Thank you for your kindness, your patience, and for simply being yourself.

Since white lilies are your favorite flowers, they had to be here.

I hope this little gift makes you smile.

I've been meaning to ask…

If you're free sometime next week, I'd really enjoy spending some time together.

Maybe coffee.

Maybe a walk.

Or just somewhere we can sit and talk.

No pressure at all.

I just thought it would be nice.

🌸

Thank you for experiencing this little surprise.

The lilies will stay here, for whenever you'd like to visit them again.
